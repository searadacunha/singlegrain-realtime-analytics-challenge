## What could break the budget

- **The analytical store.** It is the largest line and the least certain: compute units and bytes per row are [Assumed] until a load test on real events. Unbounded dashboard queries or one dedicated service per tenant would multiply it.
- **Event size.** Session-replay style payloads of 10 KB or more multiply Kinesis shards, Firehose and S3. The 5 KB billing increment of Firehose already bills a 1 KB event as 5 KB [Benchmarked].
- **Per-tenant anything.** 500 tenants times a metric, a stream, a log group or a partition: a tenant dimension on 200 CloudWatch metrics alone would cost $10,000 per month [Estimated]. Per-tenant numbers belong in ClickHouse.
- **Per-event logging.** One 1 KB log line per event is 1521 GB, about $760 per month of CloudWatch ingestion [Estimated], and it would also copy personal data into logs.
- **Egress.** Pushing every raw event to customer warehouses outside AWS costs $137 per month at $0.09 per GB [Estimated]; exports are compressed Parquet for that reason.
- **Retention in the hot store.** Storage grows linearly with months kept; the model stops at 13 months.
- **A European region.** Prices there are higher; re-run `tools/fetch_aws_prices.py --region eu-west-1` before promising a number.
